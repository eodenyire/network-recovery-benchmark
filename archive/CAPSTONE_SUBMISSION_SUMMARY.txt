================================================================================
BENCHMARK SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Network:
  • 20 nodes (PRTs) × 5 KRIs per node = 100 variables total
  • True network: 41 edges (directed, weighted)
  • Density: 10.25% (41/400 possible edges)
  • Source: data/srtg20_edges.csv

Data Generation Process:
  • VAR(1) model with autoregressive coefficients ∈ [0.6, 0.85]
  • t-copula with ν=5 degrees of freedom (tail dependence = 0.3)
  • Gaussian measurement noise

Experimental Design:
  • Time steps: T ∈ {50, 100, 150, 200, 250}
  • Noise levels: σ ∈ {0.1, 0.5}
  • Network types: Designed (W_true) + Random (Erdős-Rényi, same density)
  • Configurations: 5 × 2 × 2 = 20
  • Replications per config: 5
  • Total experiments: 100

Recovery Methods Evaluated:
  1. VAR+Diebold-Yilmaz (VAR+DY) - linear Granger causality
  2. Conditional Value-at-Risk (CoVaR) - quantile-based tail dependence
  3. Graph Neural Network (GNN) - deep learning with graph structure
  4. DebtRank (validation metric only) - contagion cascades

Evaluation Metrics (per method):
  • Precision: TP / (TP + FP)
  • Recall: TP / (TP + FN)
  • F1 Score: Harmonic mean of precision/recall
  • AUC-ROC: Area under receiver operating characteristic
  • AUPRC: Area under precision-recall curve
  • Edge-weight correlation: Pearson r
  • Impulse-response MSE: DebtRank cascade error

================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

Finding 1: Direction Identifiability is Binding
  • VAR+DY achieves 0.4998 AUC across all 10 designed-network configs
  • This is random-chance performance
  • Root cause: Only 15.4% of edges recoverable in lag channel
  • Direction identifiability diagnostic correctly predicts this outcome
  • NOT a method failure, but a fundamental DGP property

Finding 2: CoVaR Provides Robust Alternative
  • CoVaR achieves 0.5982 AUC on designed networks (20% above random)
  • Robust to noise: 0.5991 vs. 0.5973 AUC at σ={0.1, 0.5}
  • Quantile method captures tail dependence orthogonal to linear structure
  • 14 percentage-point gap to random networks (0.7025 AUC) reflects different signal

Finding 3: GNN Requires Architectural Investment
  • Current implementation: 0.2166 AUC (designed), 0.3341 (random)
  • Limitations: 5 epochs (vs. 100), hidden_dim=16 (vs. 64) due to compute budget
  • High variance (std ≈ 0.10) indicates training instability
  • Path to improvement: PRT-level graph priors, attention mechanisms, fuller training

Finding 4: Network Topology Affects Cascade Dynamics
  • DebtRank MSE: 0.1647 (designed) vs. 0.1877 (random)
  • Designed network 14% more stable for impulse-response cascades
  • Validates benchmark design; confirms true topology carries structure
  • Orthogonal metric to recovery performance

Finding 5: Data Volume Plateaus Beyond T=150
  • VAR+DY: Flat throughout (confirms identifiability constraint)
  • CoVaR: Improvement from T=50 (0.57) to T=150 (0.63), plateaus beyond
  • GNN: Slight trend upward but high noise
  • Implication: 150 timesteps sufficient for CoVaR on this DGP
